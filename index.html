About Me

A short personal story and teaching philosophy

I was born in Babougou, Koulikoro, Mali almost three decades ago. From an early age I showed interest in understanding and preserving our rich traditions — that interest grew into a passion during high school.

I studied social sciences at the École Normale Supérieure de Bamako — a decision that showed me how to share knowledge of Malian traditions and values. Today I teach Bambara to people worldwide in a friendly, culturally immersive way.

Currently based in: Budapest

Conversation

Jacori E bɛ bɔ min Bourema?
Bourema Ne bɛ bɔ Mali la. E dun?

Bourema I ni ce. Ka an bɛn.
Jacori Unse/Nba! Ka an bɛn.
Practice: Greet your partner, say your name and family name, then say where you come from.
